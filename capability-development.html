The challenge

Development budgets are often spent on the wrong gaps

Without a baseline, training is chosen by popularity or availability rather than need, and nobody can show whether it worked.

01

Gaps known anecdotally

Leaders have a sense of where the team is weak but no evidence to prioritise investment or make the case for it.

02

Generic training, limited transfer

Off-the-shelf courses rarely reflect your policies, categories or systems, so learning does not translate into practice.

03

No line of sight to outcomes

Without re-measurement, it is impossible to link development spend to better commercial results.

Our approach

A measurable path from baseline to capability

Every program starts with evidence and ends with re-measurement, so you can see the return on your investment in people.

01

Baseline

Assess individuals and teams with SkillsMap to establish a clear, benchmarked starting point.

  • Individual competency profiles
  • Team and role heatmaps
  • Benchmark comparisons
02

Prioritise

Link the gaps to your operating model and strategy so effort goes where it matters most.

  • Role clarity and alignment
  • Critical gaps by team
  • Development investment case
03

Plan

Design team and individual pathways using a 70:20:10 blend of on-the-job, social and formal learning.

  • Team learning pathways
  • Individual development plans
  • Coaching and mentoring matched to roles
04

Deliver

Run facilitated programs, eLearning and coaching tailored to your policies, categories and sector.

  • Workshops and multi-module programs
  • On-demand eLearning
  • Executive mentoring
05

Measure

Reassess and report progress so capability uplift is visible to leadership.

  • Reassessment against baseline
  • Capability reporting
  • Plan refreshed for the next cycle
