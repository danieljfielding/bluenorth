Why it matters

Procurement is the most powerful lever you have for change

With billions of dollars spent each year across public and private organisations, procurement decisions have profound consequences for communities, for the environment, and for the social enterprises and businesses that make up the supply chain.

bluenorth helps organisations move beyond compliance and tokenism to embed social value into procurement strategy, policy and practice. Whether you are responding to government requirements, meeting ESG commitments, or simply want to buy better, we help you do it well.

The challenge

Most social procurement stops at the weighting

Commitments are made and criteria are added to tenders, but few organisations can show what changed as a result.

01

Compliance without impact

Modern slavery statements and ESG criteria are in place, yet they rarely shape which suppliers win or how contracts are managed.

02

Thin supply markets

Buyers struggle to find social enterprises and First Nations businesses with the capacity to meet their requirements.

03

Outcomes that are not measured

Without agreed measures and data from suppliers, social value cannot be reported with confidence.

Our approach

Designed in, not bolted on

We work across the procurement lifecycle so social and environmental outcomes are built into decisions from planning through to contract management.

01

Set the ambition

Define what social value means for your organisation and set targets that are ambitious but achievable.

  • Social procurement strategy and policy
  • Targets aligned to government and ESG commitments
  • Executive and stakeholder alignment
02

Build the market

Map and develop supply from social enterprises, First Nations businesses and local suppliers.

  • Supplier market mapping
  • Supplier capability building
  • Partnership and subcontracting pathways
03

Embed in sourcing

Write social and environmental outcomes into category strategies, evaluation and contract clauses.

  • Evaluation methodology
  • Contract clauses and KPIs
  • Modern slavery and ESG risk screening
04

Measure and report

Track outcomes through contract management and report impact with confidence.

  • Outcome measurement framework
  • Supplier reporting
  • Social value dashboards in CPO360
