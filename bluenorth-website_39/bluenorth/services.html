Learning & Development

Build the procurement capability your organisation needs to compete — through assessment, targeted training, and expert coaching.

Effective procurement functions are built on skilled people. Our L&D offering gives organisations a structured, measurable approach to building capability at every level — from graduate procurement officers to Chief Procurement Officers.

Powered by our CPO360 assessment platform, we begin by establishing a clear baseline of current skills, then design learning pathways that address real gaps and build genuine capability.

What's included:

  • Procurement skills assessments via CPO360
  • Customised training delivery across 15+ topic areas — with on-demand eLearning available for every topic
  • Executive mentoring and coaching
  • Team-level and individual learning pathways
  • Progress tracking and capability reporting
  • Blended learning (in-person, virtual, on-the-job)

Social & Sustainable Procurement

Procurement is one of the most powerful levers organisations have to create positive social and environmental change.

With billions of dollars spent each year across public and private organisations, procurement decisions have profound consequences — for communities, for the environment, and for the social enterprises and businesses that make up the supply chain.

Blue North helps organisations move beyond compliance and tokenism to truly embed social value into procurement strategy, policy, and practice. Whether you're responding to government requirements, meeting ESG commitments, or genuinely wanting to buy better — we help you do it well.

Design a procurement function that's built for your organisation's ambitions — efficient, aligned, and built to last.
