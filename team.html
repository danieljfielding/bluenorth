Areas of Expertise

Deep specialists. Every specialism.

Our associate network spans every major area of procurement practice — so we can always field the right person for your challenge.

Cost Transformation

Identifying and delivering sustainable cost reduction across categories — from baseline analysis and should-cost modelling through to supplier negotiations and benefits tracking.

Probity & Governance

Expert guidance on probity frameworks, conflict of interest management, audit response, and ethical decision-making in both public and private sector procurement environments.

Framework Development

Designing and implementing procurement policy and process frameworks — from procurement manuals and delegation matrices to category frameworks and panel arrangements.

Supplier Relationship Management

Developing and embedding structured SRM programmes — supplier segmentation, relationship planning, performance management, and value creation beyond contract compliance.

Category Management

End-to-end category strategy development across direct and indirect spend — market analysis, sourcing strategy, supply base optimisation, and category plan execution.

Contract Management

Proactive contract lifecycle management — risk identification, performance tracking, variation management, and relationship governance to extract maximum value from every contract.

Social & Indigenous Procurement

Practical expertise in embedding social value, Indigenous procurement, and modern slavery compliance — from policy design through to supplier development and impact measurement.

Procurement Transformation

Leading large-scale procurement transformation programmes — operating model redesign, process improvement, technology implementation, and capability uplift delivered simultaneously.

Sourcing & Tendering

End-to-end sourcing execution — market engagement, RFx design and management, evaluation, negotiation, and contract award across all categories and procurement thresholds.

Spend Analytics

Extracting insight from spend data — cleansing, classification, visualisation, and analysis to identify savings opportunities, supply chain risks, and performance trends.

Construction & Infrastructure

Specialist procurement support for capital works and infrastructure programmes — head contract strategy, subcontractor management, packaging, and risk allocation.

Technology & Digital Procurement

Procurement technology selection and implementation — P2P platforms, contract management systems, spend analytics tools, and AI-enabled procurement solutions.

Stakeholder Engagement

Building productive relationships between procurement and the business — stakeholder mapping, engagement planning, business partnering models, and satisfaction measurement.

Risk & Supply Chain Resilience

Identifying and mitigating procurement risk — supplier concentration, geopolitical exposure, ESG risk, financial health monitoring, and supply chain resilience planning.

Learning & Capability Development

Designing and delivering procurement learning programmes — skills assessment, training design, coaching, eLearning development, and CPD framework implementation.

Negotiation & Commercial Skills

Building negotiation capability across teams — from structured negotiation planning and commercial modelling through to high-stakes supplier negotiation support and coaching.

Healthcare Procurement

Deep sector expertise across hospital networks, health departments, and aged care — clinical and non-clinical categories, formulary management, and value-based procurement frameworks.

Financial Services Procurement

Specialist knowledge of procurement within banks, insurers, and investment managers — regulatory requirements, vendor risk management, and complex professional services sourcing.
