Who we help

Helping purpose-led businesses reach their potential

Expert support to help social enterprises, First Nations businesses and SMEs access and win procurement opportunities, create partnerships, and build the commercial capability to grow.

Our pro bono and low bono programs are designed so the organisations that most need this expertise can get it: the same practitioners and the same rigour, at a price that works. They are funded through our commitment to invest 50% of all profits in organisations and programs working with vulnerable communities.

How it works

Practical support at the point it matters

We tailor support to where each business is on its growth journey, from first tender to strategic partnerships.

01

Assess readiness

Understand your offer, capacity and target markets, and how buyers are likely to see you.

  • Capability and readiness review
  • Target buyer and market mapping
  • Priority opportunities
02

Build bid capability

Strengthen the commercial skills and documents that win work.

  • Capability statements

  • Panel and register guidance

Grow

Ongoing mentoring as you scale, so growth is sustainable.

  • Executive mentoring
  • Contract management support
  • Governance and growth planning
