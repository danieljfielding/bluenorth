How we got here

From the front lines of procurement

BlueNorth was born from a simple belief: the best procurement advice comes from people who have done the job themselves.

After co-founding and building one of Australia's leading procurement consultancies — ArcBlue, now part of Bain & Company — Chris and Dan came together again with a clear vision. Create a boutique firm that combines deep procurement expertise with a genuine commitment to social and sustainable outcomes.

BlueNorth serves organisations across the public and private sectors, helping them build smarter, more purposeful procurement functions. Our name reflects our guiding principle: always pointing towards better outcomes, for our clients and for the communities they serve.

We combine hands-on consulting with our proprietary CPO360 platform, giving clients both the strategic guidance and the tools to make change stick.

Procurement done right doesn't just save money — it builds organisations, strengthens communities, and creates lasting value.
