Our Story

From the front lines of procurement

Blue North was born from a simple belief: the best procurement advice comes from people who have done the job themselves.

After co-founding and building one of Australia's leading procurement consultancies — ArcBlue, part of Bain & Company — Chris and Dan came together again with a clear vision: create a boutique firm that combines deep procurement expertise with a genuine commitment to social and sustainable outcomes.

Blue North serves organisations across the public and private sectors, helping them build smarter, more purposeful procurement functions. Our name reflects our guiding principle — always pointing towards better outcomes, for our clients and for the communities they serve.

We combine hands-on consulting with our proprietary CPO360 technology platform, giving clients both the strategic guidance and the tools to make change stick.

Procurement done right doesn't just save money — it builds organisations, strengthens communities, and creates lasting value.
