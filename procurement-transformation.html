Why organisations call us

Most procurement functions were never designed. They accumulated.

The symptoms tend to be the same regardless of sector, and they reinforce each other, which is why fixing one in isolation rarely holds.

01

Fragmented spend and structure

Buying is spread across business units and sites with limited coordination, so policy adherence is patchy and the organisation pays more than its buying power should allow.

02

No single view of spend or savings

Data sits in several systems with inconsistent coding. Opportunities are hard to size and claimed savings are contested by Finance.

03

Capability that is assumed, not measured

Skills gaps are known anecdotally, development spend is not targeted, and the team is asked to run strategies it has not been equipped for.

Three workstreams, one program
